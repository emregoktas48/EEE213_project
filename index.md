## Abstract — numerical problem
Charging of a series RC circuit from a DC source (problem 8), solved with the Euler and fourth-order Runge–Kutta methods and checked against the exact solution. *(In progress — Week 11.)*

## Abstract — data and model
Random forest regression of the net electrical output of a combined cycle power plant from ambient conditions (UCI CCPP data, CC BY 4.0). Test MAE 2.32 MW against a baseline of 14.72 MW. *(In progress — Week 12.)*
